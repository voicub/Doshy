# Credits and third-party data

Doshy ships no third-party data or 3D models. It fetches the following at run time, on the machine that runs the server, only when the matching feature is used.

| source | used for | terms |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) via the Overpass API | Surroundings: buildings, trees, roads, water | ODbL; the credit "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" is shown in the Surroundings panel |
| Elevation: Copernicus DEM via [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) | Surroundings: ground height | credit "Elevation: Copernicus DEM via Open-Meteo" shown in the panel |
| [AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) (Mapzen / Tilezen "Terrarium" height pictures on the AWS Open Data registry, `s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod`) | Surroundings: detailed ground height (3 to 6 m), on by default, falls back to Open-Meteo; off with `DOSHY_EXTERNAL_DATA=0` | The registry's licence is the dataset's [attribution page](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md), which asks for the sources used to be credited. The panel shows "Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles by Mapzen (USGS 3DEP and SRTM, Copernicus EU-DEM, national surveys; see CREDITS.md)". The full required list, for the places the data covers: ArcticDEM terrain data DEM(s) were created from DigitalGlobe, Inc., imagery and funded under National Science Foundation awards 1043681, 1559691, and 1542736; Australia terrain data (c) Commonwealth of Australia (Geoscience Australia) 2017; Austria terrain data (c) offene Daten Oesterreichs - Digitales Gelaendemodell (DGM) Oesterreich; Canada terrain data contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada; Europe terrain data produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union - EU-DEM layers; Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration; Mexico terrain data source: INEGI, Continental relief, 2016; New Zealand terrain data Copyright 2011 Crown copyright (c) Land Information New Zealand and the New Zealand Government (All rights reserved); Norway terrain data (c) Kartverket; United Kingdom terrain data (c) Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2015. The tiles are downloaded one by one by your server, with the User-Agent `doshy/1.0 (+https://github.com/voicub/Doshy; personal home dashboard)`, and kept on disk for 90 days. |
| [Overture Maps](https://overturemaps.org) `buildings` theme (the release's PMTiles vector tiles, found through [stac.overturemaps.org](https://stac.overturemaps.org), read from `tiles.overturemaps.org` with HTTP range requests) | Surroundings: neighbouring building footprints with measured heights, roof shapes and colours, "Buildings from: Both" by default, falls back to OpenStreetMap; off with `DOSHY_EXTERNAL_DATA=0` | The theme is ODbL ([attribution page](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/)): "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation". Its sources, with their own credits: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); Esri Community Maps contributors (CC BY 4.0); Global ML Building Footprints, licensed by Microsoft under the ODbL; Google Open Buildings (CC BY 4.0); USGS 3D Elevation Program; Qian Shi, et al., "A First High-quality Vector Data of Buildings in East Asian Countries Based on a Comprehensive Large-scale Mapping Framework", Zenodo, 22 July 2023, doi:10.5281/zenodo.8174931 (CC BY 4.0); work derived from BTN 2024, ign.es (CC BY 4.0); contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Vancouver, from the City of Vancouver, BC, Canada. The panel shows "Buildings: Overture Maps Foundation (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Esri Community Maps, Microsoft, Google and others; see CREDITS.md". Overture says its PMTiles are for inspection and may change without notice: Doshy checks everything it reads and shows "Overture data unavailable right now, showing OpenStreetMap" when it cannot. |
| Esri World Imagery (default; `SITE_TILE_URL` replaces it) | Surroundings: optional satellite photo, off by default | Esri/Maxar/Earthstar terms; credit shown with the photo (`SITE_TILE_CREDIT` overrides the text). Check your provider's terms before changing the URL. |
| [Nextzen](https://www.nextzen.org/) vector and terrain tiles (Tilezen schema, OpenStreetMap data) through the [Vectiler](https://github.com/karimnaaji/vectiler) tool, with your own free key | Surroundings: optional "City model" buildings, roads and ground height, off by default | OpenStreetMap ODbL plus Nextzen's terms; required credit "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Nextzen" is shown in the Surroundings panel while the city model is chosen; the elevation data behind the terrain tiles carries its own source credits ([Mapzen terrain tiles](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md)). Only tile positions around your home are sent. |
| [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com) Download API | optional real 3D car models, with your own token | only CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA models are offered; author, licence, source and an attribution line are stored with each model and listed under "3D model credits" in the app |
| [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) public API and asset store | optional HDRI lighting, PBR textures for floors, walls and the garden, and 3D models | the assets are CC0 (public domain): no credit is required for the assets themselves, and a plan keeps working without Poly Haven because what you pick is copied into your own database. The live API's [terms](https://github.com/Poly-Haven/Public-API/blob/master/ToS.md) ask for a clear credit: every browse dialog says "Assets from Poly Haven (CC0)" with a link, and Doshy identifies itself with the User-Agent `Doshy/1.0 (+https://github.com/voicub/Doshy; Poly Haven browser)`. Please consider [supporting them on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/polyhaven/join). The author of each asset is kept with it and shown in the app. Doshy is not made by or endorsed by Poly Haven. |
| [WashData](https://github.com/3dg1luk43/ha_washdata) | optional Home Assistant integration read by the dashboard | its own licence; nothing is bundled |

City model tool: [Vectiler](https://github.com/karimnaaji/vectiler) by Karim Naaji, MIT licence (Copyright (c) 2016 Karim Naaji). It is compiled in the Docker image from the pinned commit `f9bd89e` (the Dockerfile's `vectiler` stage) and run as a separate program; no Vectiler code is part of the app, and its licence text is in its repository (`LICENCE`). Its own dependencies, GLM and RapidJSON (MIT) and earcut.hpp (ISC), are compiled into that binary, and libcurl is linked dynamically.

Fonts: [Geist](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) (SIL OFL 1.1) through the `geist` package. Icons: [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com) (MIT).

God rays (sunbeams in the 3D view): [three-good-godrays](https://github.com/Ameobea/three-good-godrays) by Casey Primozic (Ameo), built on the [`postprocessing`](https://github.com/pmndrs/postprocessing) library (zlib licence), adapted from [goodGodRays](https://github.com/N8python/goodGodRays) by N8python. The package's `LICENSE` is the zlib licence (npm lists it as "Other"): free for any use including commercial, free to alter and redistribute, with three conditions: do not misrepresent who wrote it, mark altered source versions plainly, and keep the notice in source distributions. Doshy uses the package unmodified from `node_modules`; its two shaders are rewritten at run time for the orthographic camera and the building volume (`components/scene/godraysPatch.ts`), a change of the running program only, not an edited copy of its source. The licence is permissive and compatible with this project's MIT licence; no attribution is required, and it is given here anyway.
